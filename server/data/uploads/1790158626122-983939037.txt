SPORTS & CULTURAL EXCELLENCE SCHOLARSHIP AND ATTENDANCE CONCESSION POLICY

Section 1: Purpose and Objectives
The institution is committed to promoting extracurricular excellence in national and international sporting and cultural arenas. This policy outlines fee waivers, special internal examination provisions, and attendance credits.

Section 2: Fee Waiver Categories for Student Athletes
Clause 2.1: International Level Representation
Students representing the country in Olympics, Commonwealth Games, Asian Games, or World Championships receive:
1. 100% Tuition fee waiver for all remaining academic years.
2. Free single-occupancy air-conditioned hostel accommodation and dietary mess allowance.
3. Special sponsorship grant of INR 50,000 per tournament.

Clause 2.2: National and Inter-University Representation
Students winning Gold, Silver, or Bronze medals in All India Inter-University (AIU) tournaments or Khelo India Youth Games receive:
1. 75% Tuition fee waiver.
2. Free boarding in college residential campus.
3. Priority course and lab scheduling.

Section 3: Special Examination Provisions
Clause 3.1: Clash with Examination Schedule
If an officially scheduled tournament or national training camp coincides with Mid-Semester Assessments or End Semester Examinations, the student is permitted to appear for a Special Makeup Examination without penalty or grade cap.
Clause 3.2: Submission Timeline
The student or sports coach must submit official participation documentation to the Controller of Examinations at least 7 days before the exam commencement.
